İzin alınması durumunda dikkat edilecekler:
- Ölçeklerin resmî Türkçe çevirileri kullanılmalıdır. Sitedeki eski metinler resmî çeviri değildi.
- Ölçeğin telif notu sayfada gösterilmelidir.

## Taslak 1: Mapi Research Trust (ISI)

Başvuru ePROVIDE'daki "request" formuyla yapılır. Formun açıklama alanı için örnek metin:

> Dear Mapi Research Trust team,
>
> I am a physician (Chief Physician, VM Medical Park Bursa Hospital, Türkiye). I would like to request permission to use the official Turkish version of the Insomnia Severity Index on my personal, non-commercial health-information website (muhammedikbalbakirci.com) as a free self-assessment tool. No fee is charged, no data is stored (scoring happens in the visitor's browser), and the page states that the result is not a diagnosis. Please let me know the applicable conditions and whether a licence agreement or fee is required.
>
> Kind regards,
> Dr. Muhammed İkbal Bakırcı

## Taslak 2: Prof. Edna B. Foa (OCI-R)

Center for the Treatment and Study of Anxiety, University of Pennsylvania üzerinden gönderilir.

> Dear Prof. Foa,
>
> I am a physician in Türkiye and run a non-commercial health-information website (muhammedikbalbakirci.com). I would like to ask your permission to offer the OCI-R (validated Turkish version) as a free online self-screening tool, with full citation of Foa et al. (2002) and your copyright notice. Results are calculated only in the visitor's browser, nothing is stored, and the page clearly states that the result is not diagnostic and recommends professional evaluation for elevated scores.
>
> Kind regards,
> Dr. Muhammed İkbal Bakırcı
